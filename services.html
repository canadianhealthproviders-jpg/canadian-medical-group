Chiropractic care

A considered approach
to everyday mobility.

Explore care for concerns involving joints, muscles and the spine.

Chiropractic assessment considers your symptoms, movement and relevant health history. It provides a starting point for discussing appropriate options with your chiropractor.

Which service should I enquire about?

If you are unsure, contact the clinic and describe your general enquiry. Ask which service may be appropriate and whether an assessment is available.

Do I need a referral?

Please confirm requirements with the clinic. If you plan to claim through insurance, check your policy for referral requirements.

Can I use my insurance?

Confirm coverage with your insurer and ask the clinic about fees, receipts and billing arrangements before booking.

Is my appointment confirmed when I send a request?

A request is an enquiry. Wait for the clinic to confirm availability and appointment details.
