Which services can I enquire about?

You can contact Canadian Medical Group about physiotherapy and chiropractic services. Ask us about availability and your first visit.

Where is the clinic located?

Find us at Westwood Mall, Unit 1A07-7215 Goreway Dr, Mississauga, ON L4T 0B4.

Does an online request confirm an appointment?

An online request is an enquiry. Your appointment is confirmed only after the clinic contacts you and agrees on the details.

What should I ask before my first appointment?

Ask about appointment length, fees, what to bring and any paperwork required. If you plan to use insurance, confirm your coverage and billing arrangements in advance.
